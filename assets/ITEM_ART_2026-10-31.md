# Medicinal stones and late recovery item art

Created with the built-in image generation tool. These are project assets, not changes to game balance.
Each source was generated with a transparent background, then converted to 384 × 384 WebP with alpha preserved (quality 86, alpha quality 100). The original generated PNGs are left in the generation output directory; the game loads only the WebP assets below.

## Prompt set

Every prompt begins with this shared style specification, followed by the corresponding subject below:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single square inventory item icon for BEAST PROTOCOL, a sci-fi survival crafting game. Detailed painted game loot render, strong readable silhouette at 48px, crisp beveled edges, realistic material texture, warm orange rim light left and cool cyan rim light right, restrained fantasy glow. Three-quarter view, centered, single item occupies about 80% of square with generous safe margin. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no ground, no rectangular card, no border, no text, no letters, no watermark, no characters.

- `medical_stone`: Medicinal stone accessory: a rounded milky white and pale jade green mineral pebble with luminous green medicinal veins, a simple dark leather cord threaded through the stone so it can be worn as a charm. Natural stone, modest rare-tier charm, green healing glimmer, not a potion bottle.
- `philosophers_stone`: Philosopher's stone accessory: an exquisite crimson ruby-red faceted alchemical stone set in a compact antique gold talisman frame, subtle internal green-gold healing light, a small dark cord loop at the top. Epic-tier upgrade of a medicinal stone, angular mystical gem, no words or symbols resembling writing.
- `jewel_meat`: Jewel meat recovery food: a thick grilled slab of beast meat, appetizing browned ridges and red-pink juicy center, several small luminous multicolored gemstones naturally embedded in its surface. A single boneless portion on a small dark metal survival dish, readable meaty silhouette with distinct jewel glints, not a rock.
- `heaven_pot`: Heaven hotpot recovery food: a compact dark metal camping hotpot with two small handles, golden glowing medicinal fish broth, white fish pieces and fresh green herbs visibly floating inside, gentle wisps of steam. Three-quarter overhead view shows the delicious contents. No side dishes or utensils.
- `compressed_ration`: Compressed ration recovery food: one compact futuristic military ration brick in a partially peeled dark olive and silver foil vacuum wrapper, exposed dense golden-brown nutritious rectangular bar, small orange illuminated energy-cell-like latch on the packaging. Practical rare-tier survival food. Absolutely no printed text, numbers or logos.
- `life_elixir`: Elixir of life recovery potion: a single elegant faceted glass flask filled with radiant emerald green and golden life-restoring liquid, ornate golden laurel leaves wrapping its neck, jewel-like golden stopper and a restrained soft gold glimmer. Legendary survival alchemy potion, beautiful clear silhouette, no skulls, no readable markings.
